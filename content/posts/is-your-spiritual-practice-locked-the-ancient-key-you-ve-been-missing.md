## The Secret Behind Sacred Chants and Divine Power

Have you ever wondered why, despite reciting sacred texts like the Hanuman Chalisa countless times, you don't feel the profound change you expect? You're not alone. Many people read it, but its true power often remains locked, accessible only through a specific key. This key, according to ancient wisdom, is the guidance and grace of a true Guru.

### The Guru's Voice: The Key to Locked Mantras

There's a fascinating concept that many powerful mantras and texts are “keelit,” or locked. This isn't a flaw; it's a divine safety mechanism. The idea is that if their immense power were accessible to everyone, it could be misused by those with impure intentions, leading to chaos. The sages of old understood that great power requires great responsibility. The safeguard, put in place by them, is that these mantras only truly awaken and reveal their full potential when received from the mouth of an enlightened being—a Guru. The Guru's voice, charged with spiritual energy (shakti) and intention, acts as the key that turns the lock. It's an energetic transmission, a spiritual attunement that calibrates the seeker's consciousness to the frequency of the mantra.

Think of the renowned saint, Neem Karoli Baba. He advised everyone to chant “Ram Ram” and read the Hanuman Chalisa. While countless people follow this practice, the transformative impact was most potent for those who received the instruction directly from him. His word wasn't just advice; it was a form of initiation. It was like planting a sanctified seed in the fertile soil of a devotee's heart, a seed that was guaranteed to sprout because it came from a divine source.

A spiritual thinker I once heard beautifully described the Hanuman Chalisa as being like a daily bath. It's a cleansing ritual that anyone can and should do. This daily practice washes away the accumulated grime of negative thoughts, worldly anxieties, and subconscious fears, purifying the mind and heart. It's the foundational step to connecting with Hanuman ji's boundless devotion and strength. However, other powerful texts, like the Bajrang Baan or Hanuman Bahuk, are like potent medicines meant for specific, severe ailments and challenges—perhaps when one is facing insurmountable obstacles or spiritual attacks. You wouldn't take strong medicine daily, only when needed. The Chalisa is your daily spiritual hygiene, a universal gift from Tulsidas to all of us, preparing our spiritual body for deeper, more intensive practices.

However, the spiritual path is not static; every soul is at a different stage of its journey. It's possible that after years of dedicated chanting, a person may feel a natural urge to stop chanting Hanuman Chalisa. This feeling requires deep introspection, as it can signify two very different things. On one hand, it could be that the practice has become a comfortable refuge, and the soul is being nudged by nature to face life's lessons more directly, without a shield. On the other hand, it could be a sign of spiritual graduation. The lessons of that particular stage may be complete, and the soul is now being called to connect with higher, more abstract forms of the divine, such as the formless aspects of Shiva or the cosmic vastness of Narayana. Only then a Guru will show up in your life in one or another form and take you on the path of chanting.

## The Cosmic Dance of Shiva and Shakti

![Hierarchy of cosmic power](/assets/uploads/cddff6_75483295a084484a8c2b42c61d590fbd.png)

To understand divine power, we must understand the fundamental duality of the universe: Shiva and Shakti. Shiva represents pure, unmanifested consciousness—the silent, formless potential behind everything. He is the vast, still ocean. But Shakti, the Divine Mother, is the dynamic, creative energy that brings the universe into being. She is the waves, the tides, and the currents within that ocean. Shiva is the ultimate giver, the bestower of boons, but Shakti is the ultimate controller and distributor. She is the cosmic intelligence who decides who receives what, why, and how much. She holds the universe in a delicate, intelligent balance.

You might look at the world today and ask, “If Shakti is in control, why do corrupt people hold so much power—money, weapons, influence?” This is a misunderstanding of true spiritual power versus worldly power. The power to accumulate wealth or worldly might is governed by the intricate laws of one's karma, a system that plays out over lifetimes. This power is temporary, often binding, and carries with it the seeds of its own destruction. The power we're talking about is far greater: the power to change a life, to heal, and even to take on another's suffering. This profound, life-altering strength is not a commodity; it is a grace granted only by the Divine Mother to those who have earned it through purity, discipline, and selfless service.

This is the power of our spiritual Gurus. They possess the ability to say, “I will take your suffering upon myself.” This is not a power you can buy or seize; it is earned through immense spiritual discipline, self-sacrifice, and the unconditional grace of the Mother. It is the power to intervene in the karmic flow out of pure compassion, an act that requires an immense store of spiritual capital.

### The Truth About Miracles: The Power of Transference

What we call miracles are often acts of spiritual transference. The suffering doesn't just vanish into thin air, as that would violate the cosmic law of karma. Instead, it is consciously absorbed and processed by a spiritually advanced soul, a Siddha Purush or perfected being. They don't erase the karmic debt, but they can take it upon their own account to be paid through their physical form.

Consider the stories of Sai Baba of Shirdi. A woman came to him, crying, because her child was gravely ill. He simply said, “God is great. Go home, your child will be fine.” She returned the next day, overjoyed, saying her child slept peacefully. But she found Baba himself shivering with a high fever. He had taken the child's illness upon himself, processing the karmic debt through his own physical form.

This isn't just the stuff of legends. History has documented it. In the Baburnama, the memoir of the Mughal emperor Babur, there's a powerful account of his son, Humayun, falling deathly ill. When all the royal physicians had given up hope, Babur, who was a deeply spiritual man and an astrologer, locked himself in a room with his son for three days. He prayed, circling his son's bed, pleading with the divine for the illness to be transferred to him. This was an act of ultimate paternal love.

On the third day, Humayun rose from his bed, completely healed. At the same moment, Babur fell ill. He refused all medicine, considering it a breach of his sacred pact. He passed away soon after, having willingly given his life for his son. This demonstrates that this incredible power—the capacity to consciously take on another's karma—is the true mark of a great soul. It is this selfless ability to absorb the suffering of others that separates the enlightened from the ordinary.

### The Path to Balance: Why You Must Start with the Mother

So, how does a spiritual seeker attain this balance and connect with the divine? The advice from the wise is clear: if you want to reach Shiva, you must begin by worshipping Shakti.

The Divine Mother, Adi Shakti, is the source of all creation and the gateway to higher consciousness. Before you ask Shiva for immense power, you must acquire the strength, wisdom, and stability to handle it. Shakti worship builds the “container”—the spiritual body and nervous system—strong enough to hold the blinding light of Shiva's grace. Without a strong container, the immense energy of a spiritual awakening or a powerful boon could shatter the seeker. The Mother gives you that foundation. She is the one who tempers power with wisdom, and who ensures you won't be destroyed by the very boons you seek.

When you have her grace, you don't even need to ask Shiva directly. She knows what you can handle and what is pre-approved. There's a beautiful saying that captures this perfectly:

> Baba sab kahe, Mai kahe na koi. Baba ke darbaar mein, Mai kahe so hoye. — Everyone calls out to the Father, but no one calls for the Mother. But in the Father's court, only what the Mother says comes to pass.

The Mother is not swayed by flattery; she is a rational, discerning force. She knows that true compassion sometimes requires being firm. A mother will not give her child a drop of poison, even if he cries for it, because his well-being is her highest priority. In the same way, the Divine Mother may deny us what we want if she knows it will harm our spiritual growth. The challenges and obstacles she places in our path are her way of strengthening us. Her “no” is often a greater blessing than an easy “yes.” She can be sterner than any father if it's for her child's ultimate good.

## The Pitfall of Modern Astrology: The Problem with Quick-Fix Remedies

This brings us to a major issue in modern astrology: the obsession with “upay,” or remedies, as transactional quick fixes. The ancient texts, like the Parashara Hora Shastra, do mention remedies, but they are nuanced and deeply scientific. The prescribed remedy depends on the elemental nature of the planet causing trouble in your chart, as each element corresponds to a different way of interacting with cosmic energy.

![Planetary energy remedies based on zodiac signs](/assets/uploads/cddff6_2c5680ce35f0436d86b272414053e970.png)

Earth Element: for a planet in an earth sign (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn), its energy is dense and physical. Thus, wearing a gemstone is effective. A gemstone is a highly concentrated and structured form of planetary energy that resonates with our physical and etheric bodies, slowly stabilizing and strengthening the planet's influence over time. It's not a magic pill, but a gradual regulator.

Water Element: for a planet in a water sign (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces), its energy is emotional and flowing. Here, donating related items is the path. Afflicted water planets can cause emotional stagnation, attachment, and hoarding. The act of selfless giving breaks this pattern, allowing the stagnant emotional energy to be released and flow outwards, creating space for grace to enter.

Fire Element: for a planet in a fire sign (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), its energy is transformational and radiant. Performing a havan or fire ceremony is best. The sacred fire (Agni) is a divine messenger. When we make offerings into the fire with mantras, we are symbolically offering our negative karma to be consumed and transformed into a higher, purer vibration.

Air Element: for a planet in an air sign (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius), its energy is mental and vibrational. Therefore, chanting mantras is the prescribed remedy. Sound is vibration, and the universe is fundamentally vibration (Nada Brahma). Mantras create specific sonic frequencies that resonate with and pacify the corresponding planetary energies, calming the mental agitation and re-tuning our auric field.

The problem today is that everyone is being herded down one path. Gemstones are prescribed for all problems because they are a profitable commodity. It's no wonder they only work for about 25% of people—the ones for whom it happens to be the correct elemental remedy. We are ignoring the profound science behind the practice in favor of a one-size-fits-all, commercialized solution.

## Conclusion

True spiritual progress requires depth, patience, and correct knowledge, and it is a dynamic, evolving journey. While remedies can provide support and foundational practices like the Chalisa offer daily cleansing, the path is not static. The real transformation comes from within, through dedicated self-effort (sadhana) and a willingness to listen to the subtle shifts in one's own spiritual calling. Understanding the divine principles means not only knowing the external rules but also recognizing when your soul is ready for the next chapter, approaching each stage with reverence and sincere intention.
