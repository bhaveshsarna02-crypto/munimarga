The approach

One enquiry, carried three ways.

A chart is not a forecast. It is a description of tendency, and tendency only becomes legible when you read it against a life. That is the work on AUMAstroVisions: one graha, one house, one clear metaphor per episode.

ANTARYĀMĪ goes behind that reading to the verses it stands on. ĀNANDĀMBU steps in front of it, to the ordinary morning where restlessness or grief has to be met with something you can actually do.
